## 摘要
E-过程为随时有效推断提供了一个灵活的框架，其中传统的拒绝边界 $1/α$ 通常由 Ville 不等式给出依据。这样的边界具有普适性，但可能偏保守，因为它没有利用关于零分布的额外信息。我们提出一种参考零假设校准框架，该框架利用独立的零假设样本构造更紧的拒绝阈值，同时保持 I 类错误控制。为研究更紧阈值带来的统计增益，我们将该框架特化到共形鞅。在基于直方图的投注基础上，我们引入 Krichevsky--Trofimov 平滑，并开发用于分布偏移检测的重启混合构造。我们建立了检测功效与检测延迟的定量结果，并刻画了相对于传统 Ville 边界，拒绝边界的降低如何转化为功效与延迟增益。数值实验在一系列分布变化上验证了理论发现。最后，我们将所提出的校准策略应用于现有的在线 LLM 水印检测 E-过程，表明该方法可以在不修改底层 E-过程的情况下提升序贯检测性能。这些结果表明，参考零假设校准提供了一种通用且模块化的方式来提升基于 E-过程的序贯推断效率。

## Abstract
E-processes provide a flexible framework for anytime-valid inference, with the conventional rejection boundary $1/α$ typically justified by Ville's inequality. Such a boundary is universal but can be conservative, as it does not exploit additional information about the null distribution. We propose a reference-null calibration framework that uses independent null samples to construct sharper rejection thresholds while preserving type-I error control. To study the statistical gain from sharper thresholds, we specialize the framework to conformal martingales. Building on histogram-based betting, we incorporate Krichevsky--Trofimov smoothing and develop a restart-mixture construction for distribution shift detection. We establish quantitative results for detection power and detection delay and characterize how the reduction in the rejection boundary translates into power and delay gains relative to the conventional Ville boundary. Numerical experiments corroborate the theoretical findings across a range of distributional changes. Finally, we apply the proposed calibration strategy to an existing e-process for online LLM watermark detection, showing that the method can improve sequential detection performance without modifying the underlying e-process. These results demonstrate that reference-null calibration provides a general and modular way to enhance the efficiency of e-process-based sequential inference.
