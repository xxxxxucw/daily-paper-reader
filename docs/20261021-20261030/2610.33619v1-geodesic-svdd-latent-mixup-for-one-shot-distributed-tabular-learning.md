## 摘要
结构化数据上的分布式学习是隐私敏感领域（从金融欺诈防范到联邦医疗诊断）中一种关键且被广泛采用的方法。尽管梯度提升决策树因其高效性在集中式场景中广泛使用，但由于其不可微性，与标准的基于梯度的联邦学习协议不兼容。相反，适合联邦优化的标准深度分类器在标签分布偏斜下往往遭受灾难性遗忘。在本工作中，为弥补这些差距，我们提出 Geodesic-SVDD，一种用于分布式球面原型学习的框架，它将每个客户端的任务重新表述为超球面上的一类密度估计，从而可以在单轮中将本地模型聚合为概率流形。具体而言，对于每个客户端，我们采用残差球面编码器和 Geodesic 潜空间 Mixup，其强制执行严格的超球约束（$\|φ(x)\| \equiv 1$），防止在线性近似中观察到的流形塌缩。此外，利用这一见解，我们提出一种新颖的单轮“专家乘积”（Product of Experts）协议，无需迭代同步即可实现隐私保护聚合。最后，我们从机制上分析插值策略之间的权衡，证明尽管线性 mixup 提供了一种诱人的计算捷径，但它会使优化梯度失稳达数个数量级（$10^9\times$）。在五个表格基准和四个标签偏斜水平上，Geodesic-SVDD 在七种联邦方法中取得最高的平均 AUROC，在 Covertype 上比联邦树集成方法高出最多 21 个 AUROC 点，并且其单轮变体在 IID 和轻度偏斜下仅用一次通信轮次即可与集中式 Deep SVDD 相匹配。

## Abstract
Distributed Learning on structured data is a key and widely adopted approach in privacy-sensitive sectors, ranging from financial fraud prevention to federated healthcare diagnostics. While Gradient Boosted Decision Trees are widely used in centralized settings due to their efficiency, they are incompatible with standard gradient-based federated learning protocols because of their non-differentiability. Conversely, standard deep classifiers that are amenable to federated optimization often suffer from catastrophic forgetting under label distribution skew. In this work, to address these gaps we propose Geodesic-SVDD, a framework for Distributed Spherical Prototype Learning that recasts each client's task as one-class density estimation on the hypersphere, so that local models can be aggregated as probabilistic manifolds in a single round. Specifically, for each client, we employ a Residual Spherical Encoder and Geodesic Latent Mixup that enforces strict hyperspherical constraints ($\|φ(x)\| \equiv 1$), preventing the manifold collapse observed in linear approximations. Furthermore, leveraging this insight, we propose a novel One-Shot "Product of Experts" protocol that enables privacy-preserving aggregation without iterative synchronization. Finally, we mechanistically analyze the trade-off between interpolation strategies, demonstrating that while linear mixup offers an enticing computational shortcut, it destabilizes optimization gradients by orders of magnitude ($10^9\times$). Across five tabular benchmarks and four levels of label skew, Geodesic-SVDD attains the highest mean AUROC among seven federated methods, outperforms federated tree ensembles by up to 21 AUROC points on Covertype, and its One-Shot variant matches centralized Deep SVDD under IID and mild skew with a single communication round.
