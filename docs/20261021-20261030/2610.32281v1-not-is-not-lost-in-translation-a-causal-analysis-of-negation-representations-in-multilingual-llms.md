## 摘要
否定存在于每种语言中，但其表层实现差异巨大。尽管如此，多语言大语言模型（LLMs）能够稳健地翻译否定，这引出了一个问题：它们是否在不同语言之间共享否定的底层表示。我们在六个开放模型中，针对英语、法语、越南语和中文研究这一问题，使用对合成对比数据集以及由 FLORES-200 衍生的否定翻译数据集进行因果干预。跨模型，我们发现有力证据表明否定表示具有因果可控性，并在很大程度上跨语言共享。特别地，我们表明从一种语言中提取的对比激活方向可以在其他语言中诱导或移除否定，而从不同语言提取的方向在很大程度上对齐。我们进一步发现否定以稀疏特征表示：每个模型单个稀疏自编码器（SAE）特征就足以控制跨语言的极性，同时对否定输入选择性响应。最后，稠密和稀疏干预均能泛化到另外四种语言和 FLORES 衍生句子。我们发布数据和代码以支持未来工作：https://anonymous.4open.science/r/negation-translation/。

## Abstract
Negation occurs in every language, yet its surface realization differs drastically. Despite this, multilingual large language models (LLMs) translate negation robustly, raising the question of whether they share an underlying representation of negation across languages. We study this question in six open models across English, French, Vietnamese, and Chinese, using causal interventions on a synthetic contrastive dataset and a negation translation dataset derived from FLORES-200. Across models, we find strong evidence that negation representations are causally controllable and largely shared across languages. In particular, we show that a contrastive activation direction extracted from one language can induce or remove negation in the others, and directions extracted from different languages are substantially aligned. We further find that negation is represented in sparse features: a single sparse autoencoder (SAE) feature per model suffices to control polarity across languages, while responding selectively to negated inputs. Finally, both dense and sparse interventions generalize to four additional languages and FLORES-derived sentences. We release data and code to support future work: https://anonymous.4open.science/r/negation-translation/.
