## 摘要
本文提出了一种新颖的方法，用于估计社会科学中流行的所谓线性均值（linear-in-means）回归模型中的社会效应系数，该方法利用非实验性多维网络数据。该过程通过利用同一观测对象之间不同的、在传统意义上外生的网络链接集，可以容纳与模型误差相关的社会交互作用。具体而言，这里假设两层多路复用网络数据结构的完全可观测性，从而提出一种新的广义三阶段最小二乘（G3SLS）估计量，该估计量是一致的、渐近正态分布的，并且由于其闭式定义，使用广泛使用的现有统计软件易于实现。其基本假设足够通用，可以处理观测数据中常见的问题，如测量误差、联立性和不可观测的异质性。蒙特卡洛实验证实了所提出的G3SLS估计量在这些场景下良好的小样本性能。一项实证应用发现，在经济学顶级综合期刊上发表的研究文章之间的引用中，存在正向且显著的同伴效应。

## Abstract
A novel method to estimate social effect coefficients in the popular so-called linear-in-means regression model in the Social Sciences is presented here that utilizes non-experimental multidimensional network data. The procedure can accommodate social interactions that correlate with the error in the model by making use of a different set of network links among the same observations that are exogenous in the traditional sense. In particular, the full observability of a two-layered multiplex network data structure is assumed here to propose a new Generalized 3-Stage Least Squares (G3SLS) estimator that is consistent, asymptotically normally distributed, and also easy to implement using widely-used existing statistical software because of its closed-form definition. The underlying assumptions are general enough to accommodate common problems with observational data such as measurement error, simultaneity, and unobserved heterogeneity. Monte Carlo exercises confirm the good small sample performance of the proposed G3SLS estimator in these scenarios. An empirical application finds positive and significant peer effects in citations among research articles published in top general-interest journals in economics.
