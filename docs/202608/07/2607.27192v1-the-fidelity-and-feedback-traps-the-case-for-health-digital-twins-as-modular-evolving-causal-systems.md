## 摘要
健康领域的数字孪生可用于比较治疗方法、预测患者轨迹并支持临床决策。尽管与最初为工程应用开发的机械数字孪生相关，但在健康领域复制机械数字孪生的架构和目标可能会因两个原因而失败。保真度陷阱是一种信念，即一个准确的模型可以凭借其准确性回答假设性问题。预测和反事实推理是不同的任务，一个能很好拟合过去轨迹的孪生模型在排序治疗方法时可能会失准。反馈陷阱则出现在孪生模型基于自身推荐所帮助生成的数据进行更新时。以这种方式重新拟合可能恢复有偏的关系，并且即使数据变得稀疏，也会变得更加自信。我们认为，健康数字孪生应被构想为因果有效、模块化和演化的系统。模块化隔离了干预性推荐所需的数据和模型，因果有效性支撑此类声明，而受治理的演化则更新孪生模型，同时考虑其推荐如何重塑数据。我们得出结论，健康孪生模型的标准应是它在其帮助创造的世界中支持决策的程度，而非它对所观察世界的再现保真度。

## Abstract
Digital twins for health may be used to compare treatments, project patient trajectories, and support clinical decisions. While related to mechanical digital twins, those initially developed for engineering applications, replicating the mechanical digital twin architecture and goals may fail in health for two reasons. The fidelity trap is the belief that an accurate model can answer what-if questions by virtue of its accuracy. Prediction and counterfactual reasoning are different tasks, and a twin that can fit past trajectories well may miss the mark when ranking treatments. The feedback trap arises when the twin updates on data its own recommendations helped generate. Refitting in this way can recover a biased relationship and grow more confident even as data grows thinner. We contend that health digital twins should be conceived as causally valid, modular, and evolving systems. Modularity isolates the data and models needed for interventional recommendations, causal validity supports such claims, and governed evolution updates the twin while accounting for how its recommendations reshape the data. We conclude that the standard for a health twin should be how well it supports decisions in the world it helps create, not how faithfully it reproduces the world it observes.
