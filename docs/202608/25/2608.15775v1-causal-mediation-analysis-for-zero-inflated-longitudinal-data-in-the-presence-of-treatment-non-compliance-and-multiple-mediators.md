## 摘要
理解数字营销活动是否有效是设计有效客户参与策略的核心。我们分析了一家美国零售商开展的大规模纵向促销电子邮件活动，以评估增值激励（如免费送货）与传统价格折扣在影响客户购买行为方面的比较。由于不打开电子邮件导致的不依从、多个纵向中介变量以及零膨胀的中介变量和购买结果，分析变得复杂。为了应对这些挑战，我们开发了一个基于富集狄利克雷过程混合模型的贝叶斯因果中介框架，并使用可扩展的G计算算法估计因果估计量。我们表明，忽略电子邮件打开行为的分析会显著削弱估计效应。增值激励始终优于价格折扣，产生更高的估计潜在购买金额，且收益随时间累积。我们设计了一种个体化的序贯电子邮件策略，以在观测数据中优化预期购买次数。

## Abstract
Understanding whether a digital marketing campaign is effective is central to designing effective customer engagement strategies. We analyze a large-scale, longitudinal promotional email campaign conducted by a U.S.\ retailer to evaluate how value-added incentives, such as free shipping, compare with traditional price discounts in influencing customer purchasing behavior. The analysis is complicated by non-compliance, due to not opening emails, multiple longitudinal mediators, and zero-inflated mediators and purchase outcomes. To address these challenges, we develop a Bayesian causal mediation framework based on enriched Dirichlet process mixture models and estimate the causal estimands using a scalable G-computation algorithm. We show that analyses ignoring email-opening behavior substantially attenuate estimated effects. Value-added incentives consistently outperform price discounts, yielding higher estimated potential purchase amounts, with benefits accumulating over time. We design an individualized sequential emailing strategy that optimizes expected purchase count in the observed data.
