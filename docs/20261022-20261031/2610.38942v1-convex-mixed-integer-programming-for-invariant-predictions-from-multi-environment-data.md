## 摘要
异质数据通过揭示哪些预测关系保持不变，为识别因果预测变量提供了机会。在线性模型下，我们引入最小二乘系数与风险（LSCR）不变性原理，要求普通最小二乘系数和预测风险在不同环境间均保持不变。我们的模型既不需要指定干预机制，也不假设整个系统的结构因果模型。我们提出一种不变性得分，用于量化对 LSCR 不变性的偏离，并利用其优化构建两种互补的因果预测变量识别流程。首先，我们提出一种联合最小化预测风险和不变性惩罚的估计器，并在适当的可识别性条件下，为因果父节点的估计与恢复建立非渐近保证。其次，在不要求完全可识别性的情况下，我们开发了一种检验流程，在所有排除给定变量的预测变量集合上最小化不变性得分，从而通过乘子自助法实现具有渐近错误率控制的有效因果发现。两种流程均可表述为凸混合整数优化形式，从而避免穷举子集枚举，同时提供最优性证书。模拟和真实数据实验表明，与现有方法相比，在统计和计算方面均有显著改进。

## Abstract
Heterogeneous data provide an opportunity to identify causal predictors by revealing which predictive relationships remain invariant. Under a linear model, we introduce a least-squares coefficient-and-risk (LSCR) invariance principle, requiring both ordinary least squares coefficients and prediction risks to remain constant across environments. Our model neither specifies an intervention mechanism nor assumes a structural causal model for the entire system. We develop an invariance score that quantifies departures from LSCR invariance and use its optimization to construct two complementary procedures for causal predictor identification. First, we propose an estimator that jointly minimizes prediction risk and invariance penalty, and establish non-asymptotic guarantees for estimation and recovery of the causal parents under suitable identifiability conditions. Second, without requiring full identifiability, we develop a testing procedure that minimizes the invariance score over all predictor sets excluding a given variable, enabling valid causal discoveries with asymptotic error control via a multiplier bootstrap. Both procedures admit convex mixed-integer optimization formulations that avoid exhaustive subset enumeration while providing certificates of optimality. Simulations and real data experiments demonstrate substantial statistical and computational improvements over existing methods.
