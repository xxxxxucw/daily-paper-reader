## 摘要
我们提出了一种不确定性感知的波达方向跟踪框架，融合了深度后验估计、共形预测校准与时间平滑。该方法增强了对混响和突发性方向噪声的稳定性，相比传统波达方向跟踪方法可得到更可靠的轨迹和更强的鲁棒性。

## Abstract
We introduce an uncertainty-aware DOA-tracking framework combining deep posterior estimation, conformal-prediction calibration, and temporal smoothing. The approach enhances stability against reverberation and burst-like directional noise, yielding more reliable trajectories and greater robustness than conventional DOA-tracking methods.
